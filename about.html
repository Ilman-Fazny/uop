WHO WE ARE

A Centre for Language & Communication

The English Teaching Unit (ETU) is an academic unit within the Faculty of Engineering at the University of Peradeniya, one of Sri Lanka's foremost research universities. We are responsible for developing English language competency across all undergraduate engineering programmes.

Our team of experienced language educators works closely with engineering students from their first year through graduation, equipping them with the communication tools they need for success in academia and industry.

We take pride in nurturing not just technical communicators, but well-rounded individuals capable of creative expression, critical thinking, and confident public engagement.

WHAT WE DO

Teaching, Creating, Inspiring

English Language Instruction

Structured courses in technical writing, academic communication, oral presentation, and professional English for all engineering undergraduates.

Creative Projects

Group activities, video productions, documentaries, and art projects that develop language skills through creative and collaborative work.

Assessment & Feedback

Continuous formative assessment designed to build confidence and competence in both written and spoken English.

Cross-disciplinary Collaboration

Partnerships with engineering departments to embed communication skills in technical coursework and research projects.
